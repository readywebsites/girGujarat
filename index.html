FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Travel Queries, Answered

What is included in this Gujarat Tour package?
+

Our signature package includes comfortable group AC pushback luxury bus travel for all sightseeing and transfers, 3 nights hotel accommodation at verified pure vegetarian properties, daily breakfast and dinner, driver allowances, toll taxes, and dedicated Gir jungle safari booking assistance with forest department permit.

Can we book a private vehicle (Innova Crysta / Sedan) instead of the bus?
+

Yes, absolutely! While our standard package is organized as a comfortable group AC luxury bus tour, we readily arrange exclusive private AC cabs (Toyota Innova Crysta, Maruti Ertiga, or Tempo Traveller for families). Simply connect with us on WhatsApp for an instant customized private cab quote.

Where is the tour pickup from?
+

Complimentary pickup is provided from Ahmedabad Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport (AMD), Ahmedabad Railway Station (ADI), or any central hotel in Ahmedabad. En-route boarding at Rajkot or Jamnagar is also available on request.

Is the Sasan Gir Safari permit guaranteed?
+

Yes! We arrange official government permits and secure open 4x4 Gypsy vehicles with registered forest trackers for your morning safari in the Gir Jungle Trail / Devalia Safari Zone.

What type of accommodation and food are provided?
+

We provide verified 3-star AC hotels in Dwarka and Somnath, and a luxury resort with swimming pool in Sasan Gir. All included meals are 100% pure vegetarian, offering authentic Gujarati, Kathiyawadi, and North Indian delicacies.
